Privacy Notice

PIGEM Combined Facilities Support Services collects the personal details, service location, and valid ID images you submit only to assist with your internet service application.

  • Uploaded identification documents are stored in private storage for authorized review.

  • Application email notifications are sent only for reference numbers, correction notices, and status updates.
